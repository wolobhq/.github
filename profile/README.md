# Wolob

**Next-generation news CMS. Your news site, live in 2 minutes.**

Wolob is a fully managed publishing platform built for news publishers: local newsrooms, niche verticals, multi-site networks and the agencies that serve them. Pick a plan, choose a theme, and your site is live. No developers, no servers, no plugin stack to babysit.

## What's built in

• 20+ mobile-first themes, fast and SEO-ready  
• Google News & Discover optimisation, structured data  
• PWA with push notifications  
• AI writing assistant for drafts, headlines and tags  
• Agency feeds, ads, newsletters and memberships  
• Automatic backups, updates and security
